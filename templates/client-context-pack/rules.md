# Client Rules

## Approval gates

- External emails/DMs require human approval.
- Public publishing requires human approval.
- Paid spend requires human approval.
- Destructive data changes require human approval.

## Privacy

Never send raw prompts, completions, chats, emails, logs, secrets, credentials, tokens, cookies, headers, env values, or customer PII to the dashboard.

## Dashboard modeling

Agents are operators. Projects are workstreams/systems they operate.
